Documents Checklist
Required documents for processing. The checkboxes are for printed checklist use only.
1. Clear copy of CNIC with Original seen.
2. Two photographs
3. Application form duly filled and signed by customer along with RSM endorsement
4. KYC along with RSM endorsement
5. Last 3 months Salary slip. (Last 6 months Salary slip in case of variable salary)
6. Employment letter with Date of joining & Employment status.
7. Last 6 months Bank statement with salary reflection.
8. Previous two years experience letter, if current employment is less then 2 years.
9. Verified SS card.
